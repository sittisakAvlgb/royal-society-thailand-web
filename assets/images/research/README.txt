Research search card (home) — photo panel

Put the image in THIS folder:   assets/images/research/research-bg.png
(change the url() in assets/css/pages.css for another format)

It sits faintly behind the top of the "เผยแพร่ล่าสุด" column of the search
card below the hero (about 400 px wide on desktop), fading to white so the
list stays readable. A warm photo of a library, archive or reading room
works best (e.g. 1200 x 900 px, < 600 KB).

Until the file exists the publications photo
(assets/images/publications/publications-bg.png) stands in. When you add the
file, also add its url() line in assets/css/pages.css (search for
"research-bg.png").
Confirm usage rights with the Office.
