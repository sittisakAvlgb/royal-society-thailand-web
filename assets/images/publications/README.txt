Publications band (home) — side artwork

Put the image in THIS folder:   assets/images/publications/publications-bg.png
(change the url() in assets/css/pages.css for another format)

It is placed at the TOP-LEFT of the band, scaled to 70% of the band's height,
with a white fade from the right so the books stay readable. A soft photo or
line drawing of bookshelves / manuscripts works best (e.g. 1600 x 1000 px PNG).

Book covers: the covers are typeset in CSS. To use an official cover, add
  <img class="book__img" src="assets/images/publications/<file>.jpg" alt="">
inside the book's <a class="book__cover"> in index.html.

Confirm usage rights with the Office.
