Fellows band (home) — side artwork

Put the image in THIS folder:   assets/images/fellows/fellows-bg.png
(change the url() in assets/css/pages.css for another format)

It is placed at the TOP-RIGHT of the band behind the heading, scaled to 70% of
the band's height, with a white fade from the left so text stays readable.
A light line drawing or soft photo on a transparent/white background works
best (e.g. 1600 x 1000 px PNG, < 800 KB).

Until the file exists the band shows the light surface only. When you add
the file, also uncomment the url() line in assets/css/pages.css (search for
"fellows-bg.png" — two places: the home band and the Fellows page header).
Confirm usage rights with the Office.
